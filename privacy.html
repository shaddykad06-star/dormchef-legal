DormChef

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

DormChef is a recipe, meal-planning, and grocery app for college students. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it's used, and what control you have over it. The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work, we don't run ads, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

Your account. An email address and password, used to sign you in. You also pick a username, which is shown publicly on recipes you share.

Content you create. Recipes (titles, ingredients, steps), recipe photos, grocery lists, meal plans, and a food log of meals you record. During onboarding you can tell us your goal (e.g. eating cheap or healthy), dietary preference, and what kitchen access you have — these are used to tailor what the app shows you.

Community activity. Which creators you follow, which communities you browse, and any reports or blocks you submit through the app's moderation tools.

That's it. We don't collect your location, contacts, or device identifiers, and the app currently includes no advertising or analytics trackers.

What's public and what's private

DataWho can see it
Shared recipes, their photos, and your username Public — visible to other DormChef users, and photos are served from a public link
Grocery lists, meal plans, food log, onboarding preferences Only you
Email address and password Only used to sign you in; never shown to other users
Reports you file and users you block Only you and the DormChef team

How your data is used

  • To run the app — storing your recipes, syncing your lists and plans across sessions, and showing shared recipes in community feeds.
  • AI features. Some features send data to Anthropic's Claude API for processing: recipe text is consolidated into grocery lists, recipe pages you import by URL are fetched and parsed into structured recipes, and ingredients are matched against nutrition data. Only the recipe content needed for the feature is sent — never your email, food log, or account details.
  • Nutrition estimates. Ingredient names are looked up in the USDA FoodData Central database, a public U.S. government nutrition database.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase — hosts our database, sign-in, and photo storage.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — processes recipe text for the AI features above. Under Anthropic's API terms, this data is not used to train their models.
  • USDA FoodData Central — receives only ingredient names for nutrition lookups.

What we don't do

  • No selling or renting your data — to anyone, ever.
  • No advertising, ad networks, or cross-app tracking.
  • No analytics or profiling beyond what's described here. If we later add crash reporting to fix bugs, we'll update this policy first.

Deleting your data

You can delete your account any time from Profile → Delete account in the app. This permanently removes your account and everything attached to it — recipes, photos, saved meals, grocery lists, meal plans, food log, preferences, follows, and community memberships. There's no waiting period and nothing to email us about; it takes effect immediately and can't be undone.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS). Your private data is protected by per-user access rules enforced at the database level, so one account can never read another account's private rows.

Age

DormChef is built for college students and is not directed at children. You must be at least 13 years old to create an account.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
