Straight answers

The fine print

What is DormChef?
Cooking usually isn’t the hard part — deciding, shopping and paying for it is. DormChef takes that half: pick or import meals you’d actually eat, and it works out what to buy and roughly what it’ll cost before you spend anything.
Does it work without a real kitchen?
Whatever you’ve got to cook on. The No-kitchen filter narrows everything to meals a microwave, kettle or rice cooker can handle, so a first-year with one outlet and a housemate with a full stove are both at home here. Vegetarian, vegan, high-protein and under-500-calorie filters run alongside it.
How does the grocery list work?
Plan your week and every ingredient is merged across recipes — duplicates combined, grouped by aisle, totalled for the week, estimates marked as estimates. Set a weekly budget and lists aim for it, without ever dropping an ingredient your recipes need.
When can I use it?
It’s in development. Leave your email and you’ll hear as it progresses — the day it ships, you’ll know.
