Coming to iPhone in development

The whole week’s dinners, rung up before you shop.

DormChef is an iPhone app for college students. Pick cheap, good recipes that fit a dorm kitchen, drop them onto your week, and it merges every ingredient into one grocery list — duplicates combined, grouped for the store, totalled for the week. See the real screens below.

Occasional progress updates and word when it’s out. Nothing else, ever.
